# M6 checklist (games, and the screen-share extras): run on Windows 10 or 11

`.\dev.cmd -Prod`. For the games, have a friend or your phone on 3rtc.me in the same room. For
remote control and the screen's sound, have a second PC with the desktop app.

## Games
- [ ] Dock → Play together: Chess, Ludo, Last Card, Tycoon, Whiteboard each open, play with the phone, Minimise / Leave / End work
- [ ] Last Card: each player sees only their own hand
- [ ] Whiteboard: pen, box and line strokes show on both; the dark board and Clear work

## Host controls
- [ ] As host, turn on "Only I control playback". On the other PC, the player has no play/pause or seekbar, and no Skip or Exit; the queue has no play/next/clear; adding a video only queues it
- [ ] Turn it off: everything comes back

## Screen share extras (two PCs with the desktop app)
- [ ] The viewer clicks "Full screen" (or double-clicks the picture): it fills the monitor; the bar shows when the mouse moves; Esc leaves
- [ ] Share with "Share PC sound" and play a film or music: the other PC hears it in stereo, and doesn't hear its own voice back
- [ ] The viewer's quality menu → Sound: Off / 50% / 100% / 150% change its volume
- [ ] Share with "No sound": nothing is heard

## Remote control (two PCs with the desktop app)
- [ ] The viewer clicks "Request control": an Allow / Say no card shows here (a Windows notification too if 3RTC is behind)
- [ ] "Say no": the viewer is told
- [ ] Ask again → Allow: a red banner at the top of the shared screen says who controls; the viewer doesn't see the banner in their picture
- [ ] The viewer moves, clicks, right-clicks, scrolls and types (click the picture first): it all happens here, in the right place (also with a window shared, and on a second monitor)
- [ ] Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V and Alt+Tab from the viewer work here
- [ ] Move your own mouse a fair way (or click): control comes back to you, with a notice
- [ ] Give control again → Ctrl+Alt+Shift+X: control comes back
- [ ] "Give control" → the other PC → "Take control" on their side: they control; "Take back control" here ends it
- [ ] Stop sharing while they control: it ends; no key or button is left stuck down here

## Graphics card (a laptop with two GPUs)
- [ ] Settings → Graphics lists both cards; pick High performance → "Restart now": Task Manager → Performance shows 3RTC on that GPU
- [ ] Back to Auto → restart: Windows decides again
