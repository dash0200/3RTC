# M5 checklist (local video, sharing, screen share): run on a Windows 10 or 11 PC

`.\dev.cmd -Prod` (the first run gets the video player, `libmpv-2.dll`), and a friend or your
phone on 3rtc.me in the same room. Have an MP4 and an MKV with subtitles to hand.

## Playing a file here
- [ ] "browse files" (or the dock's video button) → pick an MKV: it plays in the stage like VLC
- [ ] The bar under it: play/pause, time, click the line to jump, mute, volume, Subtitles, Full screen
- [ ] Subtitles: the file's own tracks; "Add a subtitle file…" (.srt / .ass); Off
- [ ] Audio (a file with two audio tracks): switch between them
- [ ] Double-click the video or press F: full screen; Esc leaves it
- [ ] Drop a video file on the stage: it plays. Drop an .srt on it: the subtitles show
- [ ] The video fills the stage (no empty band on the right or bottom)

## Together
- [ ] Play / pause / jump here: the phone (with the same file, "I have this file") follows; and back
- [ ] The phone presses "Stream from …": the video plays on the phone without the file
- [ ] On the phone, jump near the end: it plays on after a moment
- [ ] An MKV with HEVC or AC-3: the phone is warned before streaming; a ⚠ line here says who
- [ ] The phone picks a subtitle track from the file: it gets the subtitles (read from the file here)
- [ ] Add an .srt here: the phone shows it too
- [ ] The phone shares a file: here "Stream from …" plays it (the bar says "Streaming · n% here")
- [ ] The phone exits: it stops here too
- [ ] A 2 GB+ file: plays here and streams to the phone (memory stays reasonable in Task Manager)

## Screen share
- [ ] Dock screen button → a list of screens and windows → pick one: the phone sees it
- [ ] Here: "You are sharing your screen" card; "Show preview" shows it; "Stop sharing" stops
- [ ] The phone shares its screen (a computer browser): it shows here; Hide / the dock button bring it back
- [ ] Laser pointer here over their screen: they see your pointer; clicks make a ripple; and the other way round

## Screen share quality (two Windows PCs with the desktop app, plus a browser)
- [ ] Share a 1440p or 4K screen to the other PC: the line under its picture says the screen's own size (e.g. 2560×1440) and about 60 fps
- [ ] Here, the line at the bottom says "GPU encoding (…your GPU's encoder…)", the other PC's name with "GPU", the browser's with "(web)" and at most 1440p
- [ ] Small text (a code editor, a spreadsheet) is readable on the other PC; scrolling stays smooth
- [ ] The other PC picks Quality: Up to 720p: the line here shows it at 720p; back to Auto: full size again
- [ ] Here, pick 1080p · 30 fps from the quality button: both viewers drop to 1080 lines (or less) and about 30 fps
- [ ] The picker remembers the last choice; so does the viewer's quality
- [ ] Task Manager > Performance > GPU while sharing: "Video Encode" is busy, the CPU stays low

## Updates
- [ ] Delete `libmpv-2.dll` beside an installed (not dev) 3rtc.exe and start it: the next update check brings it back
