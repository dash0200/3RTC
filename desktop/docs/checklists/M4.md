# M4 checklist (the watch party): run on a Windows 10 or 11 PC

Step 0 is the web view check (one small window). If it fails, stop there and send what you saw:
the rest depends on it.

## 0. Web view check
From `desktop\`: `cargo run -p rtc-app --example webview_spike`
- [ ] YouTube: a video loads under the buttons and plays
- [ ] Play / Pause / +30 s work; the line under the buttons shows `State { … }` changing
- [ ] Vimeo: same. Twitch: set `$env:RTC_SPIKE_TWITCH = "<a channel that's live>"` first, then same
- [ ] "Menu": the blue box shows. Write down whether it's **above** or **under** the video
- [ ] Hide/Show hides and shows the video; resizing the window keeps the video in place
- [ ] No crash when switching providers quickly

## Then the app
`.\dev.cmd -Prod`, a friend or your phone on 3rtc.me in the same room.

### Starting something
- [ ] The stage shows "What are we watching?" and Quick picks with thumbnails underneath
      (Trending / Music / … chips, categories, Shuffle, "+" to queue)
- [ ] "Trending in …" lets you pick a country; the list changes
- [ ] Paste a YouTube link → "Play for everyone": it plays here and on the phone
- [ ] Paste a Vimeo link, a Twitch channel, a direct `.mp4` link: each plays on both
- [ ] Paste a Netflix or Instagram link: the web app's error shows under the box

### Together
- [ ] Pause / play / seek on the desktop player: the phone follows; and the other way round
- [ ] Header: "In sync · …" pill, "Now playing · title", Skip (n/m), Exit
- [ ] Exit closes it on both
- [ ] Phone joins while a video plays: it starts at the desktop's spot
- [ ] Drag the phone's video 30 s away while both play: within ~5 s it snaps back ("Re-synced")
- [ ] Host turns on "Only I control playback": the guest's pause is undone with the 🔒 message

### Queue tab
- [ ] "+ Add" a link; queue a quick pick with "+": both show in Up Next on both sides
- [ ] ▲ ▼ reorder, ✕ remove, ▶ play now, Clear All
- [ ] A video ends: the next one starts on both. "Next ⏭" skips at once
- [ ] Skip votes: with two people one vote passes (as the web app)

### Over the video
- [ ] Reactions float in the strip to the right of the video (not hidden behind it)
- [ ] Opening a menu (More, reactions, soundboard) or a dialog hides the video while it's open;
      it comes back after (the sound keeps playing)
- [ ] Toasts show under the video's bottom edge

## In-game overlay (More → Desktop)
- [ ] Turn on "In-game overlay": a small list of who's in the call shows top left
- [ ] It turns green for whoever talks; muted people show "(muted)"
- [ ] Clicks go through it; it never steals focus from a game (borderless/windowed)
- [ ] The corner choice moves it; leaving the room hides it

## Share an app's sound (More → Share an app's sound…)
- [ ] Play music in Spotify or a browser: it's in the list, marked "playing"
- [ ] Share: your friend hears that app (and your voice); not other apps' sounds
- [ ] Mute your mic: they still hear the app, not you
- [ ] Stop sharing (or leave the room): it stops
