# M3 checklist (part 1, the audio pipeline): run on a Windows 10 or 11 PC

From `desktop\`: `.\dev.cmd -Prod` (or `-Web`). Have a friend (or your phone) on 3rtc.me in the
same room. Tick each line; anything that fails, note what you saw and send the Logs tab.

## Voice settings (More → Voice settings)
- [ ] "Classic audio" is off (our own audio path is the default)
- [ ] Mic test: speak and the bar moves; silent room, the bar stays low
- [ ] "Play test sound" plays two short notes through the chosen speakers
- [ ] Voice Isolation is on: type on the keyboard or run a fan while talking; your friend hears
      your voice but little of the noise. Turn it off: they hear the noise again
- [ ] Pick another microphone and speaker in the lists: the meter and test sound follow at once
- [ ] Outside a room, turn Classic audio on and back off: the app keeps working each way

## Voice in a room
- [ ] Your friend hears you, you hear them; no crackles, no robot voice
- [ ] Delay feels short both ways (count "one" on one side, the other repeats it)
- [ ] With speakers (no headphones): your friend doesn't hear themselves back (echo cancelling)
- [ ] Mute, deafen and push-to-talk work as before

## Per-person volume
- [ ] Click your friend's avatar in the dock: their volume card opens
- [ ] Drag the slider: they get quieter and louder (up to 200%); the badge shows the %
- [ ] 50% / 100% / 150% / 200% presets, "Mute for Me" (they go silent; a muted badge shows on
      their avatar; People says "Muted by you"), "Unmute for Me", "Reset (100%)"
- [ ] Leave and rejoin (or restart the app): their volume is remembered
- [ ] People tab: the 🔊 button on their card opens the same volume card

## Devices changing during a call
- [ ] Plug in headphones (or Bluetooth): if Windows switches to them, voice follows within a
      couple of seconds without leaving the room
- [ ] Unplug them: voice comes back on the speakers by itself
- [ ] Unplug the USB mic you picked: the default mic takes over; plug it back, it's used again

## Long run
- [ ] A 30-minute call with the window minimised: no drift, no growing delay, no glitches

# M3 checklist (part 2, the rest of version 1)

Same setup: `.\dev.cmd -Prod`, a friend or your phone on 3rtc.me in the same room.

## Chat
- [ ] The picture button sends a picture; your friend sees it, full size when tapped
- [ ] Ctrl+V pastes a screenshot (Win+Shift+S first) into the message box as a picture
- [ ] Dragging a picture file onto the window attaches it; a caption typed with it goes along
- [ ] A picture from your friend shows "Receiving … %", then the picture; click it: the
      lightbox opens, Save puts it in a folder you pick
- [ ] The smiley button opens the emoji picker; the tabs work; an emoji goes into the message
- [ ] A soft pop plays for messages, a chime when a picture starts coming

## Reactions and soundboard
- [ ] The smiley in the dock: your six reactions; clicking one floats it up for both of you
- [ ] Keys 1–6 send them (not while typing); holding a key builds a combo (×3, ×4…)
- [ ] "Choose your reactions…": add, replace, move, remove, type/paste any emoji, Reset; it's
      remembered after a restart, and keys 1–9 and 0 follow the new order
- [ ] Reactions from your friend float up with their name
- [ ] The music note: the soundboard; every clip plays for both of you; "Riya played Airhorn"
      shows over the stage; the volume steps and mute work; deafened, clips stay silent

## Host controls and connection details
- [ ] As host: More → "Only I control playback": your friend sees "Host controls" in their
      header; turn it off again
- [ ] As a guest (your friend hosts and locks): More says "The host controls playback"
- [ ] More → Connection details: round trip, "Direct P2P", data rates per person, the graph
      fills in; "Copy Diagnostic Report" copies a readable report

## Desktop
- [ ] More → Desktop → Low-power mode: reactions just fade in place; Voice Isolation says it's
      off; turn it off again: everything as before
- [ ] An old settings file (from M2) still opens with all your settings

## Releases (once 3rtc.me serves `desktop/latest.json`)
- [ ] `.\packaging\package.ps1` makes the files in `target\dist` (installer with Inno Setup 6)
- [ ] `3RTC-Setup.exe` installs without asking for admin; Start menu has 3RTC; it runs
- [ ] Settings → Apps → 3RTC → Uninstall removes it (and its Start with Windows and 3rtc:// links)
- [ ] The portable zip: unzip, run; `settings.json` appears beside the exe
- [ ] `.\packaging\package.ps1 -Publish` makes the GitHub Release `desktop-<build>`; after
      deploying the web app, `https://3rtc.me/desktop/3RTC-portable.zip` downloads that release's zip
- [ ] Publish a newer build: an older installed app finds it (More → Desktop → Check for
      updates), downloads it, and starts the new version after "Restart now"

## Version 1 budgets (Task Manager, a 4-person call)
- [ ] Memory under 150 MB; CPU under 2% with the window hidden; starts to the home screen in
      under a second
