3RTC for Windows: the watch party desktop app

Everything the 3RTC web app does, plus what a browser can’t: watch almost any video file or torrent together in sync, share your screen with its sound, let a friend take the controls, and stay in the voice call while you game. Free, no sign-up, no ads.

Download for Windows Installer, no admin rights · or the portable .zip

What only the desktop app does

  • Plays almost any videoMKV, HEVC, 4K HDR, surround sound and styled subtitles, decoded on your GPU. Switch audio and subtitle tracks.
  • Torrents and magnet linksPaste a magnet link or drop a .torrent, pick the file, and everyone watches it as it downloads. Only the file you picked is downloaded.
  • Screen sharing with soundShare a window or your whole screen with its app sound, hardware-encoded (NVENC, AMF, Quick Sync) for smooth video.
  • Remote controlLet a friend take the mouse and keyboard to help or co-play. Take control back by moving your mouse or with Ctrl+Alt+Shift+X.
  • Made for game nightsVoice and sync keep going in the background, push-to-talk works in any game, and an overlay shows who’s talking (F10).
  • Huge file transfersSend big files straight to disk, and pick up where you left off if the connection drops.

How to get started

  1. Download the installer and run it, or unzip the portable .zip anywhere and run 3rtc.exe.
  2. Create a room and share the code, or join a friend’s room, wherever they made it.
  3. Play a video file, paste a link or magnet link, or share your screen. Everyone stays in sync.

Questions

Is the 3RTC desktop app free?

Yes. It is free, with no account, no sign-up and no ads, just like the web app.

Can desktop and web users watch together?

Yes. The desktop app joins the same rooms as 3rtc.me, so friends on a phone, a Mac or a browser can join a room you made in the desktop app, and the other way round.

Installer or portable?

Either. The installer adds 3RTC to the Start menu for your user only and needs no admin rights. The portable .zip needs no installing at all: unzip it and run 3rtc.exe. Both update themselves.

Do I need the desktop app?

No. Watch parties, voice chat, live chat and Ludo all work in the browser at 3rtc.me. Get the desktop app for the extras: video files browsers can’t play, torrents, screen sharing with sound, remote control and push-to-talk while you game.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. The desktop app is for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). On a Mac, Linux, a phone or a tablet, use 3RTC in the browser at 3rtc.me.
