Movie night

Watch movies together online, with friends far away

Have a movie night with friends in other cities. Everyone watches the same movie at the same moment, talks over voice chat and reacts in the chat, like sitting on the same couch. The easiest way to watch together when you can’t be in the same room: free, no sign-up, nothing to install.

Start a watch party Free · no sign-up · works in your browser

Movie night in three steps

  1. Open 3rtc.me, create a room and send the room code to up to five friends.
  2. Pick the movie: a file on your computer (MP4, WebM, MKV) or a YouTube film or trailer.
  3. Press play. If anyone pauses for snacks, it pauses for everyone.

Long-distance friends and couples

3RTC is built for the people you can’t sit next to: friends who moved away, long-distance partners, family in another city. There’s no account to create and nothing to install, so getting everyone into the room takes one link or a 5-digit code.

Tips for a good online movie night

  • Check the file first. Pick it a few minutes early: 3RTC tells you if anyone’s browser can’t play its video or sound.
  • Use headphones if you can, or push-to-talk, so the movie’s sound doesn’t echo into the voice chat.
  • Lock playback with host controls if too many hands on the remote cause chaos.
  • Plan the next one in the queue: trailers, a short film, or the next episode.
  • Intermission: pause for snacks, play a round of Ludo, then carry on from the same frame.

Questions

How do we watch a movie together online?

One of you opens 3RTC, creates a room and shares the 5-digit code. Pick the movie file on your device (or a YouTube video), and everyone else taps Stream. Play, pause and seeking stay in sync for everyone.

Do my friends need the movie file too?

No. Only the person sharing it needs the file. It streams straight from their device to everyone in the room, starting within seconds, and it is never uploaded to a server.

Can we watch Netflix or Prime Video together?

Not inside 3RTC: Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and similar services only play inside their own apps and sites. 3RTC works with YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch, video links and movie files you have on your device. For Netflix, you can still open a 3RTC room for voice chat while each of you plays it on your own account.

Can we have subtitles?

Yes. Drop an .srt subtitle file on the player, or pick a subtitle track inside an MKV movie, and everyone gets it. Each person can change the size and timing for themselves.

How many people can join a movie night?

Up to 4 people per room. When someone shares a movie file, viewers pass it along to each other, so the person sharing it doesn’t have to upload a copy for everyone.
