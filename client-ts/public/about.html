About 3RTC

Watch together online with friends, in perfect sync

3RTC is a free online watch party app. Play a YouTube video, a Vimeo or Twitch link, or a movie file from your own device, and everyone in the room sees the same moment at the same time, while you talk over voice chat and react in the chat. It’s made for watching (and playing) together with friends who aren’t in the same room.

How it works

  1. Open 3rtc.me, type your name and tap Create a room.
  2. Share the 5-digit room code, link or QR code with up to five friends.
  3. Paste a YouTube, Vimeo or Twitch link, or pick a video file, and press play. Everyone stays in sync.

Peer to peer, by design

Most watch party services send everything through their own servers. 3RTC connects your devices directly using WebRTC, the same technology video-call apps use. A small connection server only helps the devices in a room find each other; video, voice, chat and images then travel straight between you, encrypted. That is what keeps it fast, private and free to run. See the privacy page for exactly what is and isn’t shared.

Frequently asked questions

Is 3RTC free?

Yes. There is no account, no subscription and no ads. Open 3rtc.me, create a room and share the code.

How many people can watch together?

A room holds up to 4 people. Everyone connects directly to everyone else, which keeps voice and chat fast and private, and when someone shares a video file, viewers pass it along to each other so the person sharing it doesn’t have to upload it to everyone.

Do my friends need to install anything?

No. 3RTC runs in any modern browser, on computers and phones. You can also install it from the browser menu ("Install app" or "Add to Home screen") so it opens like a normal app.

Can we watch a video file that only I have?

Yes. Pick an MP4, WebM or MKV file on your device and the others can stream it straight from you. Playback starts after a few seconds, the file is never uploaded to a server, and your friends do not need their own copy.

Does it work with YouTube?

Yes. Paste any YouTube link, including live streams, and play, pause and seeking stay in sync for everyone in the room. You can also line up a queue of videos and vote to skip.

What else can we watch besides YouTube?

Paste a Vimeo video, a Twitch channel or video, or a direct link to a video file (.mp4, .webm) or a live stream (.m3u8), and it plays in sync for everyone. You can also share a movie file from your own device.

Do subtitles work?

Yes. Drop an .srt or .vtt file on the player, or pick a subtitle track that is already inside an MKV file, and everyone in the room gets the subtitles. Each person can change the size, colour and timing for themselves.

Can we play games too?

Yes. Open Ludo from the menu: 2 to 4 players, computer players if you are short of people, and everyone else in the room can watch, with voice chat the whole time.

Is the voice chat good quality?

Voice goes directly between you at high bitrate, with echo cancellation and noise suppression that removes background noise like fans and keyboards. You can use an always-on mic or push-to-talk, and choose which microphone and speaker or headphones to use.

Does my video or voice go through your servers?

No. A small connection server introduces the devices in a room to each other, and after that video, voice, chat and images go directly between devices, encrypted by WebRTC.

Can we watch Netflix, Prime Video or Disney+ together?

Not inside 3RTC. Those services protect their videos so they only play in their own apps and sites, so no outside watch party app can play them. You can still use a 3RTC room for voice chat while each of you plays it on your own account; see the Netflix watch party page for how.

Is it good for long-distance movie nights?

Yes. That’s what it’s built for: everyone stays on the same frame, you can talk the whole time, and nobody needs an account. Pause for snacks and it pauses for everyone.

Why can’t my friend connect?

Direct connections work on most home Wi-Fi and mobile networks, but some very strict networks (some offices, schools and public Wi-Fi) block them. Trying a different network, such as mobile data, usually fixes it.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. 3RTC has a phone layout with the video on top and chat, queue and people in tabs below, plus hold-to-talk for voice.
