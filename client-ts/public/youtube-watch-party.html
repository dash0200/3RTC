YouTube watch party

Watch YouTube together, in sync, with voice chat

Paste a YouTube link into 3RTC and everyone in the room watches the same second of the same video. It’s an online watch party for YouTube: watch together with friends anywhere. Pause to talk, skip ahead, line up the next video, all while chatting by voice or text.

Start a watch party Free · no sign-up · works in your browser

How to host a YouTube watch party

  1. Go to 3rtc.me and create a room. No account needed.
  2. Send the room code or link to up to five friends. They join from any browser, on a phone or a computer.
  3. Paste a YouTube link and press play. Play, pause and seeking are synced for everyone.
  4. Add more links to the queue, or start from quick picks like lo-fi radio, synthwave and Blender’s open movies.

Why watch YouTube on 3RTC

  • Stays in syncIf someone pauses or jumps ahead, everyone follows.
  • Talk while you watchVoice chat with noise suppression, or push-to-talk.
  • Queue and vote skipBuild a playlist together and skip what nobody wants.
  • Chat and reactionsMessages, images, emoji and soundboard effects.

Good to know

Each person’s browser plays the video from YouTube itself, so everyone gets YouTube’s full quality and subtitles, and you can pick your own quality setting. A few videos can’t be embedded on other sites because their owner has turned that off; those won’t play in any watch party app, and 3RTC tells you so and skips to the next one in the queue.

Ideas for a YouTube watch party

  • Music and lo-fi radio: keep a live music stream running while you talk or study together.
  • Premieres and live streams: watch a launch, a match or a concert live with friends.
  • Video essays and documentaries: pause anytime to discuss; it pauses for everyone.
  • Reaction night: take turns adding videos to the queue and vote to skip the duds.
  • Tutorials with a friend: follow a cooking or coding video together, step by step.

Questions

How do I watch YouTube together with friends?

Create a room at 3rtc.me, share the 5-digit code or link, paste a YouTube link and press play. Everyone in the room watches the same moment, and play, pause and seeking are shared.

Is it free? Do we need accounts?

It is free, with no account, no sign-up and no ads. Your friends only need a browser.

Can we watch YouTube live streams together?

Yes. Paste the link of a live stream and everyone watches it live together.

What if one person falls behind?

3RTC checks everyone’s position every few seconds and quietly brings anyone who drifted more than a moment out of step back in line, without interrupting the others.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Open 3rtc.me in the phone’s browser; the video sits on top with chat, queue and people below, and voice works with hold-to-talk.

Why does a video say it can’t be played?

Its owner has turned off playing it on other websites (embedding), or it is age- or region-restricted. Those videos can’t be watched in any watch party app; 3RTC skips to the next one in the queue.
